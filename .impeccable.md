## Design Context

### Users
UAE audiences exploring preventive health, corporate and community programmes, research and leadership. The site will be presented to senior UAE leadership. English and Arabic are equally important.

### Brand Personality
Refined, credible, spacious. Arabian Life combines regional identity with preventive health expertise.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the approved cinematic desert hero, centred translucent navigation and UAE silhouette footer. Use ivory, forest green, sage and restrained gold. Use Arab people and UAE settings in illustrative photography; retain authentic portraits for named leadership. Avoid cramped copy, tiny type, oversized programme cards and generic heading icons.

### Design Principles
1. Compact containers, readable text; fuller content belongs on detail pages.
2. Preserve approved areas and leadership card sizes.
3. Source every research metric; distinguish historical findings from illustrative imagery.
4. Support RTL, keyboard access and reduced motion.
